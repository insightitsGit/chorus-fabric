Step-by-Step Filing Instructions
The Chorus Fabric: High-Dimensional Signal Orchestration for Machine-to-Machine Communication
File before any public disclosure. The US has a 1-year grace period for inventor's own disclosures, but Europe and most of the world do NOT. File this provisional BEFORE any demo, conference presentation, publication, or investor pitch. Once filed, you can say "Patent Pending."

Filing Steps — Patent Center

1
Go to Patent Center

Open your browser and navigate to:

https://patentcenter.uspto.gov

Click "Start a new application" or "File a patent application."
Select: Provisional Application

2
Create a USPTO Account (if you don't have one)

Click "Sign in / Register." Select "Create a USPTO.gov account." Use your email: parvaamin@gmail.com

After creating the account, return to Patent Center and start the provisional application.

3
Enter Application Data (the web form replaces Form SB/16)

Patent Center has a web form. Enter:

  • Title: The Chorus Fabric: High-Dimensional Signal Orchestration for Machine-to-Machine Communication
  • Inventor Given Name: [Your first name]
  • Inventor Family Name: [Your last name]
  • Inventor City: [Your city]
  • Inventor Country: [Your country]
  • Correspondence Email: parvaamin@gmail.com
  • Entity Status: Micro Entity (if you qualify) or Small Entity
4
Upload Documents (in this exact order)

Upload each PDF when Patent Center prompts for documents:

  1. 01_PROVISIONAL_SPECIFICATION.pdf — Document Type: "Specification"
  2. 02_PATENT_FIGURES.pdf — Document Type: "Drawings"
  3. 03_COVER_SHEET_SB16.pdf (optional — Patent Center generates cover sheet from the web form data, but you can also upload this as supplemental)
Document naming: USPTO will assign its own document identifiers. Your filenames are for your records only.
5
Claim Micro Entity Status (if applicable)

During filing, Patent Center will ask about entity status. Select Micro Entity if:

  • You have filed fewer than 4 prior US patent applications (as inventor), AND
  • Your gross income in the prior year was less than $239,754 (2025 threshold), AND
  • You have not assigned and are not obligated to assign the invention to a non-micro entity

If claiming micro entity, also upload Form SB/15A (download from www.uspto.gov — search "SB15A").

6
Pay the Filing Fee
$160
Micro Entity
$320
Small Entity

Pay by credit card directly in Patent Center. There is no surcharge for electronic filing.

7
Receive Confirmation and Application Number

After successful filing, Patent Center will display and email you:

  • Application Number (e.g., 63/XXX,XXX) — save this!
  • Filing date — this is your official priority date
  • Filing Receipt PDF — download and save
You can now publicly state: "Patent Pending — U.S. Provisional Application Serial No. 63/XXX,XXX"

Pre-Filing Checklist

  • Convert 01_PROVISIONAL_SPECIFICATION.html to PDF (Ctrl+P in Chrome)
  • Convert 02_PATENT_FIGURES.html to PDF (all 8 figures, 1 per page)
  • Fill in your name and address in 03_COVER_SHEET_SB16.html, then save as PDF
  • Verify all PDFs open correctly, are not password-protected, and are US Letter size
  • Confirm you qualify for Micro Entity status (if claiming $160 fee)
  • Download Form SB/15A from USPTO website (if claiming Micro Entity)
  • Have credit card ready for payment in Patent Center
  • Create USPTO account at patentcenter.uspto.gov
  • Confirm no public disclosure of the invention has occurred before filing
