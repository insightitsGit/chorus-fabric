  - PDF only
  - Font embedded (no external font references)
  - No security/encryption on the PDF
  - Paper size: 8.5 x 11 inches (US Letter) or A4
  - Margins: minimum 3/4 inch on all sides (1 inch preferred)
  - Font: 12 point minimum, preferably Times New Roman or Arial
  - Line spacing: 1.5 or double-spaced
  - Page numbers: bottom center, starting at page 1
  - Line numbers: optional but recommended (every 5 lines)

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER FILING:
===========================
  1. You receive an Application Number and Filing Receipt by email immediately.
  2. Your priority date is established as of the filing date.
  3. You have 12 months to file a Non-Provisional Application claiming
     priority to this provisional.
  4. The provisional is NEVER published — it remains confidential.
  5. You can now say "Patent Pending" on your product/documentation.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT publicly disclose the invention before filing.
  The US has a 1-year grace period for inventor's own disclosures,
  but most international jurisdictions (Europe, China) do NOT.
  File BEFORE any public demo, conference presentation, or publication.

NEXT STEPS AFTER PROVISIONAL:
  Month 1-6:  Validate with potential licensees/customers ("Patent Pending")
  Month 6-10: Decide whether to file non-provisional (full patent)
  Month 11:   File Non-Provisional Application (costs ~$800-1,600 filing fee
              + patent attorney fees $5,000-$15,000 if using counsel)
              OR file a PCT International Application (~$4,000) to protect
              in EU, China, Japan, etc.

ESTIMATED TOTAL COST:
  Provisional only (DIY):              $160-320
  Non-provisional (DIY):               $800-1,600
  Non-provisional (with attorney):     $6,000-$16,000
  PCT international (with attorney):   $10,000-$25,000
